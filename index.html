ABOUT THE APPLIANCE

A PRACTICAL
FOUNDATION.

Comone's IP-based PA hardware appliance is a 2U rack-mount platform for basic PBX applications and call center operations.

Built with standard, globally available components, it supports local maintenance and includes database backup and restore. Higher-capacity storage and additional LAN cards can be specified when ordering, at an additional cost.

01 / PLATFORM

Rack-ready hardware

The 2U metal cabinet comes with a rack-mounting kit. The listed build includes a PowerX or equivalent H81 motherboard, an Intel i3 below 5th generation, DDR3 PCL RAM and a Fingers or equivalent Mini ATX power supply.

02 / STORAGE

Capacity for recordings

The default 256 GB NVMe SSD is Crucial or equivalent. The source quotes up to 40,000 hours of conversation recordings; a higher-capacity drive can be specified when ordering at an additional cost.

03 / NETWORK

Onboard + PCI Express

An onboard network card and a gigabit, single-port PCI Express LAN card are listed for VPN-based SIP trunks from Indian telecom operators. Extra cards for trunks from different providers must be specified when ordering and cost extra.

04 / WORKLOAD

Know the stated limits

The appliance is listed for basic PBX applications with up to 2,000 extensions and call center operations with up to 25 agents. Confirm suitability for your actual workload with the supplier.

05 / MAINTENANCE

Service and restore

Standard, globally available components support local maintenance. The appliance also includes a database backup and restore option; confirm the backup procedure for your deployment.

06 / BEFORE YOU ORDER

Confirm the configuration

RAM capacity, the exact processor model and power-supply wattage are not stated in the source. Confirm these, equivalent components and the price of any larger drive or additional LAN cards with the supplier.

DEPLOYMENT GUIDANCE

FOR CRITICAL APPLICATIONS

For mission-critical operations, or predictive dialing or ACD with more than 25 agents, Comone recommends using your own high-performance hardware.
