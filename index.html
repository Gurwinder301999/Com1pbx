NETWORK CONNECTIVITY

READY TO
CONNECT.

The appliance includes an onboard network card and an additional PCI-based network card, suitable for VPN-based SIP trunks from Indian telecom operators. Extra LAN cards can be added for multiple SIP trunks from different providers.

ADDITIONAL LAN CARDS MUST BE SPECIFIED WHEN ORDERING AND ARE AVAILABLE AT AN EXTRA COST.
